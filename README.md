# NextLeap-Milestone-1-2026

## Milestone 1 - ChatGPT : Increase the Usage of Voice Input on the ChatGPT Mobile App

**Week 1: Mapping Business Outcome to Product Outcome**

### Understanding the Market

You have recently joined the ChatGPT India mobile team. In your first week, your manager has given you a project to work on: increase the usage of voice input on the ChatGPT mobile app.

While voice input is available on the app, very few users in India seem to be using it. Your manager believes that voice input could be a powerful tool, especially in a mobile-first country like India, but adoption has remained low. Your first task is to understand the landscape and figure out why.

To get started, your manager has asked you to complete the following in the first 2 weeks:

1. Understand voice input usage trends in India: how do Indian users interact with voice features on other apps (e.g. Google Assistant, WhatsApp voice notes, Alexa)?

2. Analyse the ChatGPT mobile experience: where is voice input surfaced? Is it discoverable, intuitive, and contextually helpful?

3. Identify 2-3 competing or analogous products and evaluate how they integrate voice interactions.

4. Understand why increasing voice usage could be valuable for ChatGPT - and outline potential product outcomes.

Given this is your first project, you are looking to make a good impression. So, get cracking! Your work in Milestone 1 will chart out the path forward for the future milestones.

You need to submit a 3-slide deck:

Slide 1: Your understanding of the Indian voice-tech and mobile usage landscape

Slide 2: Insights from analysing ChatGPT’s current UX and comparable voice-first apps

Slide 3: Why this product outcome matters and a KPI tree for increasing voice usage on ChatGPT

Points to keep in mind:

1. You can use tools like Google Slides, PowerPoint or even tools like Figma, Canva, Pitch (or any other tool of your choice) to create the slide. You can either directly save it as a PDF, or export it as image and add it to your deck.

2. Please cite the appropriate sources while mentioning any relevant data points (if any).

Finally, don’t worry if you seem a little confused or feel challenged at the moment. Productive struggle is part of the learning process, and by the end of the week - you should have a lot more clarity on how to solve this.

One day at a time.

Your goal for this week - Submit the first Milestone by Friday in the 2nd week!
